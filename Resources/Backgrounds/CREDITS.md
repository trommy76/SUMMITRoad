# Ride card background photos

- `SummitRoad_1.jpg`, `SummitRoad_2.jpg`: supplied by the project owner, who confirmed they can be bundled. They are darkened and desaturated when drawn.

You can add your own photos (.jpg or .png) to `~/Library/Application Support/SummitTrainer/backgrounds/`; if that folder has any, they are used instead of the bundled ones.
